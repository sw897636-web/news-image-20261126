A single 1024×1024 infographic summing up the world's news of **Thursday 26 November 2026** — an entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261126).

## The six stories

1. **US · TRADE — US PLANS NEW TARIFFS ON 14 TRADE PARTNERS** — The Trump administration cites "structural overcapacity" in a new Section 301 move. China faces the highest rate; Taiwan up to 15%. S&P 500 futures fall 2.1%, Nasdaq 100 down 3.1%.
2. **MIDDLE EAST · ENERGY — HOUTHIS CLAIM STRIKE ON SAUDI REFINERY** — Yemen's Houthis claim an attack on Ras Tanura, Saudi Arabia's largest refinery (550,000 barrels a day). The coalition says 4 drones were shot down; no casualties reported. Brent crude +3.5% to $94.61; WTI +2% to $83.68.
3. **COLOMBIA — CARTAGENA HOSTAGES ALL FREED** — After a ~17-hour standoff at an Exito supermarket, security forces storm in and free every remaining hostage. One gunman is killed; 10 people were freed earlier.
4. **SOUTH AFRICA — COURT RULES RAMAPHOSA IMPEACHMENT REPORT UNLAWFUL** — A South African court rules the impeachment report against President Ramaphosa unlawful.
5. **SPACE — FIRST FOREIGN ASTRONAUT ABOARD CHINA'S SPACE STATION** — Pakistan's Muhammad Zeeshan Ali arrives aboard Shenzhou-24 — the first foreign astronaut to visit Tiangong.
6. **USA · THANKSGIVING — THANKSGIVING TURKEY PARDON** — At the White House ceremony, a turkey named Feathers runs off into the bushes instead of taking the stage. VP JD Vance serves troops Thanksgiving meals in Ohio.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to the output directory (`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, default `/output`). The copy is byte-for-byte, so two runs produce identical output. `render.py` (Pillow, DejaVu fonts) draws the infographic from the verified facts listed above.

## Files

- `main.ts` — the entry program
- `entry.png` — the 1024×1024 infographic (139,750 bytes)
- `render.py` — the script that draws `entry.png`
